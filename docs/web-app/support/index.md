# Support

Have a question, hit a bug, or want to suggest a feature? Start with the FAQs, then write to us.

## Check the FAQs first

The [FAQs](faqs.md) cover the questions we get most often: what optimization means and how Sympheny applies it, how the demand profiles are generated, how to share a project, and why a result sometimes differs from what you expected.

## Contact us

For everything else, the fastest way to reach us is the [Sympheny support portal](https://urbansympheny.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/2). Raise a request there for support questions, bug reports, and feedback on new or existing features.
