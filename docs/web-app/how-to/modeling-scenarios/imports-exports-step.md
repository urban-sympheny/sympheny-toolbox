# Imports & exports step

In this step, you define the energy your scenario can purchase from outside the
system (imports, such as grid electricity or natural gas) and the energy it can
sell or send out (exports). For background, see the
[Imports](../../concepts/imports.md) and [Exports](../../concepts/exports.md)
concept pages.
