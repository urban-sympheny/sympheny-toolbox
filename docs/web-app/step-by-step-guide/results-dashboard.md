# Results dashboard

The interactive results dashboard is where you explore and share the results of a scenario execution.

## Choose what to view

Three selectors in the top right corner of the dashboard set what the charts show:

- **Solutions**: which optimal solution to view.
- **Stages**: which stage to view.
- **Hubs**: which hub to view.

Select several solutions to compare them against each other, or a single solution, stage, and hub to see that combination in detail.

## Detailed views

A single solution, stage, and hub gives you:

- The Sankey diagram of energy flows.
- The energy diagram of installed capacity.
- Bar charts of investment costs and capacity.
- Monthly energy flows.
- Daily and hourly energy flows.
- Storage state of charge.
- Load curves.
- The self-sufficiency matrix.

## Work with the charts

Every chart is interactive:

- Filter the content from a drop-down menu, or by clicking entries in the legend.
- Double-click a legend entry to show only that item.
- Zoom in, change the resolution, and move parts of a chart.
- Download the data table behind a chart, or the chart itself as a high-resolution image.
