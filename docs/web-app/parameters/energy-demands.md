# Energy demands

| Parameter | Definition | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Energy Demand Name | The unique name used to identify the energy demand within the scenario. | n/a | n/a |
| Hubs | List of hubs where the energy demand occurs. Each demand must be linked to at least one hub. | n/a | n/a |
| Stages | List of stages during which the demand is active. Each demand must be assigned to at least one stage. | n/a | n/a |
| Energy Carrier | The energy carrier of the energy demand. | n/a | n/a |
| Energy Price | The price of the energy used to meet the demand. A positive value represents a revenue, while a negative value represents a cost. | CHF/kWh | n/a |
| Profile Name | The name of the hourly profile that defines the energy demand. | n/a | n/a |
| Reverse | Indicates whether the demand is considered to be negative, i.e., whether the system consumes energy rather than produces it. | n/a | False |
