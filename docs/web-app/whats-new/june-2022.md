# June 2022

## How to model specific technologies

A new section has been added to the user guide with detailed explanations of how to model specific Technology candidates, such as:

- Heat Pumps
- Electrical Chiller
- Reversible Heat Pump
- Groundwater Heat Pump
- Geothermal Heat Pump
- Wastewater Heat Pump
- Free Cooling
- Ice Storage

## Sustainability labels

In [scenario results](../step-by-step-guide/execution.md#execution-history), you can now find relevant sustainability labels achieved by your optimized energy system. More labels will be added soon.
